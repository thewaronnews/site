The current chapter · United States · 18 September 2026

The White House bars reporters from CNN, MS NOW and Politico

The latest access ban in a record that begins in 1900. Governments in nine other countries have used the same tactic.

Donald Trump, the president of the United States, announced on 18 September 2026 that three news organisations would be barred from the White House, the president's official residence and office. The next day the Secret Service, the federal agency that protects the president, deactivated the outlets' hard passes (the standing credential that lets a reporter enter the White House press area) and turned their reporters away at the gates.

The president described the outlets' coverage as "FICTION and LIES". The White House has not said which story or stories prompted the ban.

"The Constitution's press protections do not depend on whether the president likes a news organization's coverage."White House Correspondents' Association, the organisation of reporters who cover the president, 18 September 2026

On 20 September, other television networks declined to fill CNN's place in the press pool, the small group of reporters who cover the president in rooms too small for the full press corps. CNN, MS NOW and Politico sued on 21 September in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia, a federal trial court in Washington, the capital of the United States.

Hong Kong convicts Apple Daily founder Jimmy Lai under the National Security Law

  • Detention and violence
  • Secrets and espionage laws
  • Outlet licensing and closure

Jimmy Lai, the Hong Kong media owner who founded the Apple Daily newspaper, was convicted on national-security charges on 15 December 2025 and sentenced to 20 years in prison on 8 February 2026. The charges were brought under the National Security Law, a 2020 law imposed on Hong Kong by China's national legislature.

Head of government: John Lee, Chief Executive of Hong Kong · Acting authority: Hong Kong Department of Justice
