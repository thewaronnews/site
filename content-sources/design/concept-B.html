The White House bars reporters from CNN, MS NOW and Politico

USUnited States · Americas · national government18 September 2026

Donald Trump, the president of the United States, announced on 18 September 2026 that three news organisations would be barred from the White House, the president's official residence and office. The next day the Secret Service, the federal agency that protects the president, deactivated the outlets' hard passes (the standing credential that lets a reporter enter the White House press area) and turned their reporters away at the gates.

The president called the outlets' coverage "FICTION and LIES". The White House has not said which story prompted the ban.

"The Constitution's press protections do not depend on whether the president likes a news organization's coverage."White House Correspondents' Association, the organisation of reporters who cover the president, 18 September 2026

Hong Kong convicts Apple Daily founder Jimmy Lai under the National Security Law

  • Detention and violence

Jimmy Lai, the Hong Kong media owner who founded the Apple Daily newspaper, was convicted on national-security charges on 15 December 2025 and sentenced to 20 years in prison on 8 February 2026, under the National Security Law, a 2020 law imposed on Hong Kong by China's national legislature.
