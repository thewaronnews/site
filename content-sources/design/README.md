# The War On News: three homepage concepts (2026-09-22)

All three share the same content: the masthead and scope line, search with filter hints, the current chapter (White House ban of CNN, MS NOW and Politico, United States, September 2026), three ways in with counts, a tactic by country comparison, the 1900 to 2026 timeline, recent coverage, a specimen incident with its cited sources, and the v2 footer. Counts and pre-2020 rows are placeholders; each HTML file lists them in a comment at the top.

## A. Broadsheet (`concept-A.html`)
Reads like a newspaper of record. A centred nameplate sits over a double rule, with the sections in one ruled row. The comparison matrix is the hero table: tactic rows, country columns, and each cell gives the year, the event, the leader and the outcome. Below it the current chapter runs as a lead story in two columns with a drop cap and a pull quote. Recent coverage sits in a side column. The page closes with ways-in lists using dot leaders and a bar-chart decade strip.
- Fonts: Playfair Display (display, 600 to 900) with Source Sans 3 (humanist sans text, 400 to 700)
- Palette: paper #F7F4EC, panel #EFEADF, ink #16140F, secondary ink #4A443B, soft rule #CFC7B6, accent oxblood #9E1B1B (tint #F3E3DD), reversed green #1F5B3A

## B. Atlas (`concept-B.html`)
Starts from place and leads with data. The hero pairs a headline count with a search panel and filter chips. Beneath them is a world tile map, one square per country, shaded by number of incidents, with the United States outlined as the current chapter. Five continent cards follow. The current chapter is a card with a facts grid and a list of the same tactic in other countries. Ways in use bars, tactic chips and a decade histogram, and the timeline is a horizontal strip. Country codes appear as text badges, and all numerals are tabular.
- Fonts: Inter Tight (headings, 600 to 800) with Inter (text, 400 to 700, tabular numerals)
- Palette: background #F2F5F8, surface #FFFFFF, ink #0E1A2B, secondary #475569, lines #D5DCE5, map scale #E3E8EE / #BFCBD8 / #8DA2B9 / #536C88 / #243B5A, warm accent #B93A0A (tint #FBEDE6)

## C. Dossier (`concept-C.html`)
Styled as a case file. The header is a folder label with index tabs for navigation. The hero is a vertical spine from 1900 to today, with one anchor per decade, and it ends in the 2020s entries. Beside the spine, the current chapter is pinned as a docket sheet with a margin line and a "Sourced" stamp. Recent coverage is laid out as dated clippings, and the ways in are tabbed index cards. The comparison is a compact monospaced ledger with outcome codes (O, R, U, S). The specimen entry shows the "Archived" and "Superseded" stamps.
- Fonts: IBM Plex Serif (headlines and text, 400 to 700) with IBM Plex Mono (dates, dockets, labels, 400 to 600)
- Palette: desk #EAE3D4, paper #F7F3EA, sheet #FFFDF8, folder #DCCFB4, ink #1F1B16, secondary #554C3E, rule #B5A88E, stamp red #A3231B (tint #F7E6E1), stamp grey #5B564D

All text colour pairs were checked at 4.5:1 or higher (WCAG AA). All pages were rendered at 1440 px and 390 px with no horizontal overflow.
