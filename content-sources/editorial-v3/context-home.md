This site records one kind of pressure on journalism: government action that limits reporters' access, credentials or funding. That pressure runs alongside an economic one. Newsroom employment in the United States has fallen 26% since 2008, and newspaper advertising revenue is down about 80% since 2005 [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/fact-sheet/newspapers/), while misleading content, cheaper to produce, spreads faster than verified reporting.
